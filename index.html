Common Questions

What families ask before enrolling.

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

What's the difference between the 8-week and 12-week programs?

The 12-week program includes everything in the 8-week program plus four additional sessions, a second full practice ACT, advanced strategy coaching, and a college admissions strategy meeting.

Can we start with just the Assessment?

Yes. The Assessment stands on its own and includes a program recommendation — many families use it to decide before committing to a full program.

What if my student's score doesn't improve?

Every program is built around measurement, not hope — a midpoint and final practice ACT show real movement, and the plan is adjusted along the way instead of waiting until the end to find out it isn't working.

Why do programs cost more than hourly tutoring?

A program includes a full diagnostic, a personalized plan, practice ACTs, progress reports, and parent updates built in — not just seat time. It's priced for a defined outcome, not by the hour.
